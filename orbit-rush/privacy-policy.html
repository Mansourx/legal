Orbit Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Orbit Rush ("the game") is made by Ahmad Mansour, an independent developer based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell personal information.

Reminders (daily challenge, streak, weekly event) are local notifications scheduled on your device. No data leaves the device for them, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Your choices

  • Ads consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the game asks for consent before using data for ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy Choices. On iOS, you can also refuse tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Analytics: you can reset your device's advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Deletion and access: email lowkey9.ai@gmail.com with your request. Because analytics data is pseudonymous, include the install ID shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen. We reply within 30 days. Progress saved in iCloud or Google Play Games can be deleted from your Apple or Google account settings, and uninstalling the game deletes the local save.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services

These providers process data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

International transfers and retention

Data processed by Google may be stored in the United States or other countries. Analytics data is kept for 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. This includes rights under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), the EU and UK GDPR, and US state laws such as the CCPA. To use any of them, email us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
